# Cinderella Gray — Source Timeline

Source continuity: `CINDERELLA_GRAY`.

Core arc begins with Oguri Cap in the Kasamatsu environment and moves toward Central/Tracen competition, with Belno Light, Jo Kitahara, Fujimasa March, Tamamo Cross, Symboli Rudolf and many other named characters.

Fanfic treatment deliberately **does not** place this in the real 1980s. Compatible events are compressed into 2025, immediately preceding the 2026 main-story opening, so Oguri/Rudolf remain plausible active students/racers.
