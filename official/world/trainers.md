# Official Research — Trainers and Staff

Official animation sources establish named/credited trainer and staff roles, but different adaptations do not necessarily use one identical trainer model.

## High-confidence animation roles
- **Trainer / Spica Trainer** — anime-original central trainer for Team Spica; official S1/S2 cast pages credit him simply as `トレーナー`.
- **Hana Toujou** — Team Rigil trainer in the TV anime.
- **Tazuna Hayakawa** — Tracen administrative/support figure recurring across media.
- **Trainer Okita** — Narita Top Road's trainer in RTTT.
- **Trainer Tanabe** — trainer figure in Beginning of a New Era, associated with Jungle Pocket and prior Fuji Kiseki context.
- **Jo Kitahara** — Kasamatsu-side trainer/mentor in Cinderella Gray.
- Additional named trainers/staff are indexed in `official/characters/INDEX.md`.

## Fanfic-sensitive unknowns
Exact legal employment category, contract mechanics, disciplinary reach and medical/race veto power vary or are under-explained. Do not state a universal rule until adopted under `fanfic/world/racing-rules.md`.
